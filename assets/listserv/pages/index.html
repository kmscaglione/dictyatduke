Dicty ListServ

The Dicty ListServ is an email discussion list where everyone can post questions and answers related to Dictyostelium by writing to: dicty@listserv.it.northwestern.edu. In order to receive the answers that people send to the ListServ users need to become a dictyBase colleague.

We have complied a ListServ Archive, with all the questions and answers sent to the Dicty ListServ since 1997.
